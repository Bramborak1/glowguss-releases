How it works

From music to a finished show in four steps

Upload your music, pick the moods, watch the preview and download. That's all the work there is.

Music

Upload the tracks you want for your Aufguss. Glowy analyses them and finds the beats, bars, silences and the places where someone speaks in the recording.

Mood

I split the show into parts and give each one a light mood. If you write your story, Glowy suggests the moods to match it.

Preview

Play the music and watch your own sauna in 3D. Anything you don't like, you change with a click or by dragging on the timeline.

Download

Save the finished show straight into QuickDMX or download it as a zip. Scenes, timeline and music are all ready.

Features

Built for the sauna, not the disco

General lighting software knows nothing about stoves, benches and 90 degrees. Glowguss does.

On the real beats

No fixed-tempo loops. The lights follow the beats of your music and even recognise a waltz in 3/4 and tempo changes.

Glowy AI follows your story

Write a few lines of your script. Glowy suggests a mood for each part and effects on exact moments, such as a crash, a blackout or sparks. You can give him the full script of your theatre show and he prepares the lights for it.

3D preview of your sauna

See the show before your guests do. The preview brightness can be matched to how your sauna really looks.

Over 25 moods

From Quiet Dusk through Northern Lights to Thunderstorm. Every button is a small living sauna, so you see what you pick.

Take a show to another sauna

Load a finished QuickDMX show and convert it to another sauna's lights. Guest appearances and competitions without programming from scratch.

Your music stays with you

Tracks are analysed right on your device and never uploaded. Only your shows (moods, timings and light settings) are backed up to our server under your account.

What Glowy watches

A few rules, otherwise a free hand

Glowy sticks to a few rules that are easy to forget when programming by hand. Beyond that, every show is unique.

“A few rules I always keep. The rest is your show.”
Glowy, the Glowguss mascot
  • No colour chaosEach scene keeps one colour idea and accents are saturated, so the atmosphere stays.
  • The stove stays calmStove lights glow warm and change only slowly, they never flash. Guests always have a calm point, even when the rest of the sauna is alive.
  • Every light has its roleWalls create the atmosphere and change smoothly. Rhythm and beats are played mainly by the LED strips on the benches, so it's lively without the whole sauna flashing.
  • Beats only where the drums playDuring speech and silence the lights quietly hold the mood.

FAQ

What sauna masters ask

Do I need QuickDMX?

Yes. Glowguss builds shows for QuickDMX (The Lighting Controller), the most common lighting software in saunas. Save the show straight into its folder or download it as a zip.

My sauna has different lights. Will it work?

Yes. You set up your sauna once: either load your QuickDMX project or pick the lights by hand (walls, benches, stove, water, spots). Shows are then built exactly for it.

Do I need to know AI?

No. You can build a show without AI just by picking moods. Glowy AI is the easier way: write your story and get a design in moments.

What if I don't like the design?

Edit it. Change a part's mood with a click, move or stretch parts by dragging, and everything snaps to the real beats. Manual edits are free.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, you can design and preview a show on your phone. You then save it into QuickDMX from the computer it runs on.

Is my music uploaded anywhere?

No. Music is analysed right on your device. Only the analysis (where the beats and parts are) goes to the server to build the lights.

Who is behind Glowguss?

A sauna master from Prague who performs theatrical Aufguss ceremonies and didn't want to spend his evenings programming lights. Glowguss grew out of his own shows.
